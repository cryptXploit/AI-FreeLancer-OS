**Companion Chapter:** Chapter 8 — *Async Client Updates & Revision Minimization*  
**Edition:** Tier 2 Interactive Workbook & Tier 3 Complete Freelancer Operating System  
**Operational Goal:** Eliminate unscheduled check-in calls, protect 90-minute morning Maker Blocks, and reduce client revision cycles to <= 1.2 rounds per deliverable.

---

## How to Use This Operational Worksheet

Use **Part A** every Friday at 1:30 PM to dispatch your 3-Bullet Status Pulses across all active accounts, **Part B** to storyboard your 3-Minute Walkthrough Video before shipping any milestone deliverable, **Part C** to build your Guided Feedback Frame (and translate vague feedback into concrete specs), **Part D** to log unscheduled call deflections and revision rounds, and **Part E** to verify your weekly deep-work continuity.
