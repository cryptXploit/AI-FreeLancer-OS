# Worksheet 6.1: The 20/60/20 Sandwich Execution & 4-Gate Quality Assurance Workbook

**Companion Chapter:** Chapter 6 — *AI-Assisted Project Execution & Quality Control*  
**Edition:** Tier 2 Interactive Workbook & Tier 3 Complete Freelancer Operating System  
**Operational Goal:** Cut deliverable production time by 45% to 60% while eliminating generic AI output, factual hallucinations, and multi-round client revision loops.

---

## How to Use This Operational Worksheet

Duplicate this worksheet for every major client deliverable you produce. Complete **Part A** and **Part B** during the first 20% of your production time block (Human Strategic Architecture), log your modular execution in **Part C** during the middle 60% (AI Heavy Lifting), and complete **Part D** and **Part E** during the final 20% (Human Editorial Polish & 4-Gate Pre-Submission Quarantine).
