## 1. Publishing Platform Rules & Commercial Mechanics

### 1.1 Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP)
* **Print Trim & Bleed Specifications:** Standard trade paperback trim size locked at **`6.0" x 9.0"` (`152.4 x 228.6 mm`)** with **No Bleed**. Minimum calculated gutter margins are set to `0.75"` inside and `0.625"` outside/top/bottom (`300 DPI` high-contrast vector assets) so diagrams and tables never clip during KDP print-on-demand trimming.
* **Kindle eBook Royalty Band (70%):** Priced at **`$9.99 USD`** inside the `$2.99–$12.99` 70% royalty eligibility window across US, UK, Canada, and Australia marketplaces (net of minimal Kindle delivery cost per MB).
* **Print Paperback Royalty Band (50%–60%):** Priced at **`$16.99 USD`**, capturing the 60% standard Amazon marketplace royalty rate minus fixed per-page print manufacturing costs.
* **KDP AI Content Disclosure Policy:** Amazon KDP distinguishes between *AI-Generated* (autonomous machine creation with minimal human oversight, requiring mandatory disclosure on the KDP Content tab) and *AI-Assisted* (human-architected, human-edited works where AI tools assist in brainstorming, structuring, or editing, which do not require an AI-Generated flag). Our studio maintains transparent documentation under `book-config.yaml` classifying the manual's human-governed engineering methodology.
* **KDP External Hyperlink Compliance:** Per Amazon KDP Kindle External Links Guidelines, links inside an eBook may direct readers to complimentary, value-adding ancillary materials (such as printable PDF checklists and companion worksheets) provided they:
  1. Do not link directly to competing eBook storefronts or commercial shopping carts;
  2. Do not require a mandatory purchase or gated obstacle for basic book comprehension; and
  3. Include descriptive anchor text clearly stating the destination resource.
* **Cross-Border Payout Rail:** Royalties are disbursed via Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT / ACH) directly into a **Payoneer US Virtual Receiving Account** with zero wire check minimums.

### 1.2 US–Bangladesh Income Tax Treaty Compliance (Article 12 — Royalties)
* **Statutory Default vs. Treaty Rate:** Without tax treaty relief, the United States Internal Revenue Service (IRS) mandates a default **30% statutory withholding tax** on U.S.-source publishing royalties earned by non-resident aliens.
* **Article 12 Relief (10% Reduced Rate):** Under **Article 12 (Royalties) of the United States–Bangladesh Income Tax Convention**, residents of Bangladesh who submit a valid **IRS Form W-8BEN** inside the Amazon KDP, Draft2Digital, or Gumroad tax interview using their valid **Bangladeshi 12-Digit Taxpayer Identification Number (e-TIN)** in the Foreign TIN field reduce U.S. royalty tax withholding from **30% down to 10%**.

### 1.3 Gumroad Direct Commercial Engine (Tiers 0, 2, 3 & 4)
* **Fee Structure:** **$0.00/month** fixed platform cost; **10% + $0.50 USD** flat transaction fee on direct sales via custom links or landing pages (30% only on organic Gumroad Discover Marketplace transactions).
* **Multi-Version Product Architecture:** Supports hosting **Tier 0 (`$0+` Pay-What-You-Want Free Lead Magnet)**, **Tier 2 (`$24.00` Interactive Workbook Edition)**, and **Tier 3 (`$49.00` Complete Freelancer Operating System)** inside a unified product page or dedicated funnel listings with automatic version-specific file delivery.
* **Recurring Membership / Subscription Engine:** Supports **Tier 4 (`$9.00/month` or `$49.00/year` Living OS & Pro Vault Pass)** with automated recurring billing, member-only workflow drops, and license key management.
* **Payout Rail:** Direct local bank transfer (BDT) or USD disbursement via **Payoneer**.

### 1.4 Draft2Digital (D2D) Wide EPUB Distribution (Tier 1 Wide)
* **Distribution Network:** Free automated aggregation of the Reflowable **EPUB 3.0** edition (`$9.99 USD`) to **Apple Books, Rakuten Kobo, Barnes & Noble Press, Everand (formerly Scribd), OverDrive, CloudLibrary, Odilo, Palace Marketplace, and Baker & Taylor** public library networks.
* **Commission & Payout Threshold:** D2D retains roughly 10% of the retail list price only when a copy sells (`$0` upfront fee) and disburses earnings via **Payoneer** once accrued royalties reach the **$20.00 USD** minimum payout threshold.

### 1.5 Deferred Platforms in Phase 1 (Etsy & Payhip)
* **Etsy Deferred:** Bangladesh is currently excluded from the domestic **Etsy Payments** supported countries registry. Opening a new Etsy seller shop without a registered foreign business entity (such as a US LLC or UK Ltd) and matching banking credentials triggers automated onboarding blocks.
* **Payhip Deferred:** Payhip relies exclusively on direct **Stripe** or **PayPal** merchant integrations for instant checkout—neither of which supports native merchant onboarding for standard domestic Bangladeshi personal accounts without foreign corporate formation. Both Etsy and Payhip are deferred until post-launch foreign entity expansion.

## 2. Empirical Market Findings & Practitioner Friction Data

1. **The Administrative Time Leak & Effective Hourly Rate (EHR) Collapse:**
   * Operational time-tracking audits across independent digital service providers (1–5 years experience, `$2,000–$8,000/month` gross revenue) consistently demonstrate that **35% to 40% of total weekly working hours** are consumed by unbilled administrative friction: custom proposal writing, chasing client credentials, untangling vague revision feedback, and ad-hoc inbox triage.
   * When unbilled admin and scope creep hours are included in the denominator (`Actual EHR = Gross Revenue ÷ Total Real Working Hours`), a nominal `$60–$75/hr` billing rate routinely collapses to an actual Effective Hourly Rate of **`$22–$28/hr`**.
2. **Scope Creep & Unpriced Revision Loops:**
   * Independent practitioner surveys and agency operations threads (including longitudinal community analyses in `r/Freelancers` and `r/consulting`) identify informal "quick favors" in Slack, email, and WhatsApp as the single largest source of margin destruction—costing solo operators **6 to 10 unbilled hours per week** (`$1,200–$2,400/month` in lost billable capacity).
3. **Client Onboarding Delays (The 14-Day Asset Bottleneck):**
   * Projects that sign without a structured 48-hour credential and asset gate experience an average **7-to-14-day kickoff stall**, compressing delivery timelines and creating artificial weekend emergencies for the freelancer.
4. **Prompt-Dump Fatigue vs. Variable-Driven Operational Prompts:**
   * Market reviews of generic AI prompt packs (e.g., "500 ChatGPT Prompts for Freelancers") reveal rapid buyer abandonment because one-line prompts lack context slots, role constraints, output schemas, and human quality-assurance rules. Practitioners pay a premium for **50 deeply engineered, variable-driven workflow prompts** tied directly to specific lifecycle gates.
5. **Notion Template Over-Complication Backlash:**
   * Community critiques across `r/Notion` highlight severe user fatigue with over-engineered, 20-database aesthetic templates that require hours of weekly maintenance. *The AI Freelancer Operating System* counters this directly with a **5-Database Minimalist Notion OS** (*Leads CRM, Active Projects, Client Portal, Prompt Vault, Weekly CEO Review*) built strictly for sub-15-minute weekly upkeep.
