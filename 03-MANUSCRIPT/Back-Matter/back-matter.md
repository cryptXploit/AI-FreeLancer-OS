```text

Thanks so much for reaching out about [Project Topic]—I took a quick look at [Client Company] and love what you're building with [Specific Detail].

1. Primary Outcome: What is the #1 measurable result this project needs to achieve in the next 60–90 days?
2. Target Timeline: Do you have a specific launch date or external event driving the schedule?
3. Budget Alignment: Our fixed-fee productized sprints for [Service Type] start at $[Minimum Tier 2 Floor] (and our standalone Diagnostic Blueprints start at $[Tier 1 Price]). Does that align with the investment range you have allocated for this phase?

As soon as you reply with a quick "Yes" on those three bullets, grab any time that works for you on my calendar here: [Booking Link].

Best,
[Your Name]
```
