```text
- Diagnostic Sprint Fixed Fee: [E.G., $950 USD — 100% CREDITED TOWARD FULL BUILD IF APPROVED WITHIN 30 DAYS]
- Target Full Implementation Range: [E.G., $4,800 TO $7,500]
- Sprint Turnaround Window: [E.G., 7 BUSINESS DAYS FROM ASSET ACCESS]

EXECUTION INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Draft a concise, executive-ready 1-page Paid Diagnostic Sprint Proposal containing:
   - Section A (The Cost of Blind Execution): Explain in 3 sentences why quoting a fixed price before auditing their live stack forces agencies to pad quotes by 40% or cut corners.
   - Section B (4 Concrete Diagnostic Deliverables): Define 4 tangible, high-value artifacts the client keeps whether they hire us for Phase 2 or not (e.g., 1. Technical & UX Friction Tear-Down Audit, 2. Prioritized 90-Day ROI Roadmap, 3. Exact Work Breakdown Structure [WBS] & Fixed-Price Phase 2 Quote, 4. 15-Minute Executive Loom Walkthrough).
   - Section C (The 100% Credit Guarantee): State clearly that 100% of the [Diagnostic Sprint Fixed Fee] is credited directly toward Phase 2 if they greenlight the build within 30 calendar days.
   - Section D (48-Hour Access Checklist): List the exact 3 read-only credentials needed to start Day 1.
2. Keep tone calm, analytical, and peer-to-peer. Avoid all generic sales hype.
```
**Verification Rule:** Confirm that all 4 diagnostic deliverables are standalone tangible assets the client can hand to any engineer or designer, and verify that the 30-day 100% credit expiration date is explicitly stated.
