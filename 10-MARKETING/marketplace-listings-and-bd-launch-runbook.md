## Section 4: Verified Bangladesh Seller Tax (10% Treaty Rate) & Cross-Border Payout Runbook

This runbook documents the verified banking and tax configuration for digital product publishers operating from **Bangladesh** selling into the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia.

### 4.1 Step 1: Obtaining Your 12-Digit Bangladeshi e-TIN Certificate
Before starting the Amazon KDP or Draft2Digital tax interview, ensure you have your **12-digit Electronic Taxpayer Identification Number (e-TIN)** issued by the **National Board of Revenue (NBR), Bangladesh** (`https://secure.incometax.gov.bd/`):
1. Your legal name and residential address on your e-TIN certificate must match the exact spelling on your **National ID (NID) / Passport**, your **Payoneer account**, and your **Amazon KDP / Draft2Digital tax profile**.
2. Do **not** leave the Foreign Tax Identifying Number (FTIN) box blank during the US tax interview. Leaving it blank invalidates treaty relief and triggers the default **30% US IRS statutory withholding rate** instead of the **10% treaty rate**.

### 4.2 Step 2: Connecting Your Payoneer US Virtual Receiving Account to KDP & Draft2Digital
Neither Amazon KDP nor Draft2Digital sends direct ACH royalties to domestic Bangladeshi BDT bank accounts without expensive wire thresholds. Instead, route both platforms through **Payoneer Global Payment Service**:
1. Log into your verified **Payoneer** account (`https://myaccount.payoneer.com/`).
2. Navigate to **Receive $\rightarrow$ Global Payment Service** and click on your **USD (`$`) Receiving Account** (typically issued by First Century Bank, Citibank, or Community Federal Savings Bank).
3. Copy these four credentials:
   - **Bank Country:** `United States`
   - **Account Type:** `Checking`
   - **9-Digit ABA Routing Number:** `[Your Payoneer USD Routing Number]`
   - **Account Number:** `[Your Payoneer USD Account Number]`
4. Paste these credentials into **Amazon KDP (`Account -> Getting Paid -> Add Bank Account -> United States`)** and **Draft2Digital (`My Account -> Payment Settings -> Payoneer / US Direct Deposit`)**.
5. For **Gumroad (`Settings -> Payouts`)**, select either **Direct Local Bank Transfer (Bangladesh — BDT)** using your local bank's SWIFT/routing details OR connect your **Payoneer USD account**.

### 4.3 Step 3: Line-by-Line IRS Form W-8BEN Tax Interview Walkthrough (Claiming the 10% Rate)
When completing the online Tax Questionnaire inside **Amazon KDP** or **Draft2Digital**:

| Tax Interview Prompt | Exact Entry for Individual Publisher in Bangladesh | Operational Reason |
